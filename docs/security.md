# Security

What the cockpit defends, against whom, and what is left to the app.

**Status:** the cockpit is not built yet. Each section below is written when
the part it describes is built; until then it only says what it will cover.

## Whom the cockpit defends against

Who can reach a cockpit on a person's machine, and which of them it keeps
out.

## The link's token

The token in the link a person opens, how the browser client keeps and sends
it, and what happens without it.

## Opening beyond loopback

What the cockpit accepts by default, and what an app takes on when it opens
it to other machines.

## The rules an app writes: deny and ask

How an app tightens what the agent may do, and what it cannot loosen.

## The agent's sandbox

What the agent can reach on the machine while it works.

## What stays out of reach

What the cockpit does not protect against, so that an app can decide what it
must do itself.
